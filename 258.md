\# Inventory Management Software



\## Feature 22: Batch/Lot Tracking



Batch/Lot Tracking allows an inventory management system to group and monitor products according to their specific batch or lot number. This helps businesses identify where products came from and track them throughout the inventory process.



\### Benefits



\- Helps identify products belonging to a particular batch or lot.

\- Makes it easier to trace products during inventory movements.

\- Helps businesses manage products with different production dates.

\- Supports product recalls by identifying affected batches.

\- Improves inventory accuracy and traceability.



\---



\## Feature 25: Product Supplier Information



Product Supplier Information allows the system to store details about the suppliers associated with each product. This makes it easier for users to know where products are sourced from and how to contact the supplier.



\### Benefits



\- Keeps supplier information organized.

\- Allows products to be linked to their suppliers.

\- Makes it easier to contact suppliers when restocking is required.

\- Helps users identify the source of products.

\- Improves purchasing and inventory management.
