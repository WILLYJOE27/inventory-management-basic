# Feature 1: Smart Reorder Suggestions

What it does: The bot looks at how fast an item sells and suggests when and how much to reorder. The manager can approve a draft purchase order in the chat.

Example chat: "Bot: Rice sells about 30 bags a week and you have 40 left. Reorder 100 bags from Supplier A? Reply YES to approve."

Why it matters: Stops both stock-outs and over-ordering, so money isn't tied up in unsold goods.

# Feature 2: Expiry Date Warnings

What it does: The bot tracks expiry dates and warns staff about items that will expire soon, so the oldest stock is sold first.

Example chat: "Bot: 20 cartons of milk expire in 5 days. Move them to the front shelf."

Why it matters: Reduces waste and losses on perishable goods.
