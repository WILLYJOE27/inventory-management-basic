# Barcode Scanning

Barcode scanning is an inventory feature that uses barcode scanners or mobile cameras to read product barcodes and automatically identify items in the system.

## Benefits

- Reduces manual data entry errors.
- Speeds up receiving, picking, counting, and shipping.
- Updates stock in real time.

## How It Works

- Each product has a unique barcode or SKU.
- The user scans the barcode.
- The system finds the product and updates the quantity.

# Batch and Expiry Date Tracking

Batch and expiry date tracking records the batch/lot number and expiration date of products.

## Benefits

- Reduces expired stock waste.
- Helps with product recalls.
- Supports FIFO/FEFO.

## How It Works

- When stock is received, the user enters batch number and expiry date.
- The system stores the batch details with the product.
- Reports and alerts show items nearing expiry.
