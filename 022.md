1. Batch & Expiry Tracking: Keeps records of product batch numbers, manufacturing dates, and expiry dates.

Benefits:

-Helps prevent expired products from being sold.

-Makes it easier to trace defective products.

-Reduces product waste.

-Helps prioritize products that expire sooner.

2. Stock Transfer Management: Records when inventory is moved from one store, warehouse, or location to another.

Benefits:

-Prevents loss of items during transfers.

-Shows where each item is currently located.

-Makes stock movement easier to monitor.

-Helps maintain accurate inventory records.
