\# Inventory Management Software



\## Feature 1: Stock Transfer



Stock Transfer allows users to move inventory from one warehouse, store, or location to another while keeping the inventory records updated.



\### Benefits



\- Keeps track of products moved between locations.

\- Updates stock quantities at the sending and receiving locations.

\- Helps prevent inventory discrepancies.

\- Makes it easier to manage stock across different locations.

\- Provides a record of inventory movements.



\---



\## Feature 2: Multi-Warehouse Management



Multi-Warehouse Management allows a business to manage inventory stored in multiple warehouses or locations from one system.



\### Benefits



\- Allows users to view stock across different warehouses.

\- Helps businesses monitor inventory at each location.

\- Makes it easier to transfer products between warehouses.

\- Provides better visibility of total inventory.

\- Helps businesses determine where products are available.
